# 22-Week Learning Retrospective

## Why This Matters

You've just completed a 22-week journey through JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, SQL, and data engineering. A retrospective captures what you learned, what you built, and what's next. This is your career story — the narrative that hiring managers and future collaborators want to hear.

## What You Built

Your Personal Learning OS is a full-stack web application with:

- **Authentication** (NextAuth credentials provider)
- **Database** (PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM, 12 models)
- **Dashboard** with XP tracking, streak, and weekly score
- **Roadmap** showing 2 tracks, 12 modules, 72 lessons
- **Lessons** with checkpoint questions and spaced repetition
- **Assignments** with submission system and rubrics
- **Notes** with Markdown editor, tags, and pinning
- **Study Log** tracking daily hours, mood, and energy
- **Analytics** with Recharts visualisations
- **Portfolio** aggregating all completed work
- **AI Coach** panel (ready for API integration)
- **Deployed** to Vercel with Supabase Postgres
